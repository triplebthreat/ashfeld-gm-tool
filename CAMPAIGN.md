## Campaign Structure

Two-act arc. **Act 1 (levels 1–2)** and **Act 2 (levels 3–4)**, with a natural
break point where the party either confronts Corvyn and realizes they're
outmatched, or has a collaborative conversation with him where he explains they
aren't ready to deal with Sevryn. The break must emerge from player
understanding and agency — never a forced event.

## Cosmology & Factions

- **The Stranger** — a departed god of secrets, crossroads, and mystery. Inner-sect
  theology holds that magic itself is the Stranger's scattered essence.
- **The Waking Accord** — a faith whose mainstream reveres ancient concentrations
  of magic as holy sites. Its **inner sect** believes those sites can be *harvested*
  to force the Stranger's return — sacred work, in their eyes. Sevryn is inner-sect.
- **The Autumn Court** — fey nobility. Exiled Corvyn for disrupting a cycle the
  court had decreed (likely one meant to end something the court wanted ended).

## Ashfeld (starting location)

A declining logging town of 50–100 people on a forested borderland edge.
Economy built on logging, now fading. Quiet, close-knit, wary of outsiders in
the way small dying towns are. *(Town map: `Maps/Ashfeld/Ashfeld Village Map.png`)*

**Places:** Ashfeld Manor (the ruling family's home) · Guard Post (run by the
Crane family) · Crane Household (servant family to the Ashfelds) · Old Logging
Camp on the outskirts (Sevryn's base — placement provisional).

## NPCs

### Lord Edric Ashfeld (Corvyn) — town lord · Autumn Court fey exile · *Neutral*
Currently inhabits the body of a 10-year-old boy. Genuinely pleasant, warm,
well-liked, territorial about Ashfeld.
**Secret:** Centuries-old fey nobility, exiled from the Autumn Court. Self-anchored
to the land Ashfeld sits on as a survival mechanism. His form cycles through ages
with partial-to-no control — currently stuck at 10 years old and at diminished
magical capacity until the form matures. Magic is non-mortal in flavor: subtle
autumnal wrongness, things that quietly *happen* rather than spells being cast.
History of quietly handling people who got too close to the truth (incidents
cluster around the grandfather generation). Investigating Sevryn because the
rituals threaten his anchor. Genuinely loves the town — moral complexity, not a villain.

### The Adoptive Father — father of "Edric," fully human · *Neutral*
Raised by Corvyn's *previous* form (raised his own "father" without knowing it).
Devoted, ordinary. **Secret:** knows nothing.

### The Mother — married into the family · *Neutral*
**Secret:** knows what Edric really is; carries it alone (possibly shared with the
Crane family head — undecided).

### The Elder Sister — ~12–13 years old · *Neutral*
Never told the secret, but instinctively knows something is wrong with her brother.
**Secret:** no hard knowledge — subtext channel for observant parties.

### Crane Family Head — head of the servant household · *Neutral*
Loyal, discreet, real weight in town matters. **Secret:** knows everything about Corvyn/Edric.

### Captain of the Guard — Crane family · *Neutral*
Practical, protective. **Secret:** knows Edric isn't what he appears, not the full truth.

### Sevryn — warlock, functional antagonist · *Hostile*
Conducting rituals causing visible harm around Ashfeld. Corvyn is investigating him.
**Secret:** True believer, not a mercenary. Inner-sect Waking Accord — has done the
theological math: Corvyn is a holy site, and harvesting his essence can force the
Stranger's return. Believes it's sacred work. In tension with the Accord mainstream
that *reveres* the sites his sect exploits.

## Quests

### The Harm in the Woods — Investigation · Act 1 (levels 1–2) · **Available**
Sick livestock, blighted forest patches, unsettled townsfolk. Traces to Sevryn's
rituals, unknown to the party at the outset.
**DM note:** entry point to the Sevryn thread. Keep early clues ambiguous — Corvyn
neither implicated nor exonerated.

### What Lord Edric Is — Mystery · Act 1–2 · **Hidden**
The slow-burn mystery of the town's lord. Surfaces through the sister's unease,
grandfather-generation rumors, Corvyn's slips.
**DM note:** resolves over sessions via environmental storytelling, not one roll.
The act break lands when the party understands why Sevryn threatens Corvyn specifically.

## Campaign Drop Format

When prep in a chat produces new content, end by asking for a **campaign drop**:

```
## CAMPAIGN DROP
### New NPCs
- Name | role | location | attitude | public description | secret/DM notes
### New Quests
- Name | type | tier | location | status | description | DM notes
### New/Changed Locations
- Name | where (existing town? new map?) | description | DM notes
### Canon Changes
- (anything that modifies existing NPCs, quests, or lore — say what changes and why)
### Loose Threads
- (ideas mentioned but not committed to canon)
```

Rough notes work too — the drop format just makes ingestion faster and safer.
